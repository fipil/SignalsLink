1.1.1
Fixed: A chute or damper at a side block of a multiblock crate (Multiblock Crates mod) now keeps the crate to one kind of item, like at the main block. Before, it put anything in.
Tweak: `target firepit` on the bottom block of a distillation boiler builds the firepit under it and then feeds logs while it holds less than six hours of fuel; the Igniter lights that firepit too.
Tweak: The condenser and the bottom block of the fruit press are the bucket's place to a chute or damper: it sets a bucket down there and takes it away with its contents, with conditions on the liquid as when collecting from the ground. Nothing else goes into those blocks that way; the condenser's water still comes by hose.
Tweak: The top block of the fruit press is its mash: a chute or damper puts fruit in (`amount N` as a batch) and takes the mash out, wet before pressing and dry after it. New paper actions `do press` and `do release` turn the screw down and up, and the condition `isPressing` tells whether it is down. The handbook explains it under Managed Chute and Paper Conditions.
Tweak: New paper condition `firepitReady`: true when the firepit at that end is lit or ready to be lit (a boiler's when cold or lit, a firepit block's when burning or holding fuel; a burnt-out one waits for a log). With `!isBurning` it is the edge for the Igniter.
Tweak: A valve attached to the condenser or to the bottom block of the fruit press treats the bucket standing there as an ordinary bucket: it fills it and draws from it (the bucket is slot 2).
Tweak: New block Clock: three output pins carry signal time as Phase (16 per day), Turn (16 per phase) and Beat (16 per turn), 4096 steps a day, put on the pins at once when placed or loaded. Twelve lights under its glass bulb form a figure per digit and glide to the next one. The handbook explains it under Clock.

1.1.0 - published 2026-10-14 20:00
Fixed: Ctrl + right click with an item on a device no longer writes the item's hint (code and properties) over the paper conditions on the spot. It opens the same confirmation dialog as a paper, on every device, even one without conditions; Ctrl is also sprint, and a log in hand over a ceiling damper used to wipe the orders (Thanks to TodosGame).
Tweak: New paper directive `recipe <product> [N][+|-]`: lays a crafting-grid recipe out in the first nine slots of the target (a crafting machine, any 9+ slot inventory) from the source, N multiples at once, topping up only what is missing and leaving a tool from the last craft in place. The handbook explains it under Paper Conditions.
Tweak: A paper paragraph that is only `output N` is now a block that always holds: at the bottom it is the fallback value of the Output pin, higher up it overrides the output blocks below it. Before, such a paragraph was silently dropped.
Tweak: Paper sections can be switched by the Input pin: end a header with `when 3` (or `when 1 2 4-6`; a bare `when 2` header means the device's own ends). While Input holds one of the values the section transports continuously, other sections are ignored, and Input stops meaning a batch or 15 on such a paper. One `when` means every section needs one. Works on chutes, dampers, valves and the dock; the handbook explains it under Paper Conditions.
Tweak: A storage yard now needs only 2 tiles instead of 4.
Tweak: Chutes and dampers now reach the inventory of a multiblock through any of its blocks, not only the main one; a damper hung on such a block loses its legs as on a chest.
Tweak: A block can keep automation out through chosen faces (used by Signals Machines for machine doors).
Tweak: Bottomless chest: Shift + click on a filled slot with an empty cursor clears just that slot (pattern and contents).
BEWARE, possible breaking change in `keep`: when a paper block also names a target slot (`target N`, `target last`, or the Target pin), `keep N` now counts only what lies in that slot, not the whole target inventory. Before, `target 3` + `keep 5` filled slot 3 until the chest as a whole held 5; now it holds 5 in slot 3. Papers without a target slot are unchanged. Check papers that combine the two: the level you meant for the whole container must now be written without `target`, or split per slot.

1.0.5 - published 2026-09-28 12:57
Fixed: While the chunk anchor is still counting its load, the info box says so instead of showing two unreadable squares.
Fixed: Looking at a pin of the chunk anchor now shows only the pin name, like on the other blocks. Before, the pin name was glued to the end of the anchor's own text.
Fixed: A firepit lit by the igniter now keeps igniting fuel by itself, like one lit by a torch. Before, it burned one log and then looked lit while giving no heat.
Tweak: The managed chute embedded in a wall can now switch its room sealing with Ctrl + wrench (cooling wall, insulating wall, open), like the ceiling damper and the wall sleeve coupling.
Tweak: New paper condition blockTemperature>N (also >=, <, <=, =): the temperature of the block at that end, for firepits, ovens, forges and other heated blocks.
