1.0.7
Tweak: A storage yard now needs only 2 tiles instead of 4.
Tweak: Chutes and dampers now reach the inventory of a multiblock through any of its blocks, not only the main one; a damper hung on such a block loses its legs as on a chest.
Tweak: A block can keep automation out through chosen faces (used by Signals Machines for machine doors).
Tweak: Bottomless chest: Shift + click on a filled slot with an empty cursor clears just that slot (pattern and contents).
BEWARE, possible breaking change in `keep`: when a paper block also names a target slot (`target N`, `target last`, or the Target pin), `keep N` now counts only what lies in that slot, not the whole target inventory. Before, `target 3` + `keep 5` filled slot 3 until the chest as a whole held 5; now it holds 5 in slot 3. Papers without a target slot are unchanged. Check papers that combine the two: the level you meant for the whole container must now be written without `target`, or split per slot.

1.0.6 - published 2026-09-28 20:45
Fixed: The entity sensor can be charged with a temporal gear again. Before, the right click started the gear's own "set spawn" action instead.

1.0.5 - published 2026-09-28 12:57
Fixed: While the chunk anchor is still counting its load, the info box says so instead of showing two unreadable squares.
Fixed: Looking at a pin of the chunk anchor now shows only the pin name, like on the other blocks. Before, the pin name was glued to the end of the anchor's own text.
Fixed: A firepit lit by the igniter now keeps igniting fuel by itself, like one lit by a torch. Before, it burned one log and then looked lit while giving no heat.
Tweak: The managed chute embedded in a wall can now switch its room sealing with Ctrl + wrench (cooling wall, insulating wall, open), like the ceiling damper and the wall sleeve coupling.
Tweak: New paper condition blockTemperature>N (also >=, <, <=, =): the temperature of the block at that end, for firepits, ovens, forges and other heated blocks.
