1.1.1
Fixed: The ticket's chat messages come in the player's language, not the server's.
Tweak: A ride with "wait" keeps the train held by the ticket, boiler out: board and pick the next destination, release it with the ticket from anywhere, or call it elsewhere. It waits TaxiWaitMinutes (signalslink.json, default 5), then leaves as after a ride; so does a train whose holder leaves the game, and one left waiting across a server restart. "Wait for me" at a pickup has the same limit. Needs Signals Link 1.1.2.

1.1.0 - published 2026-11-15 10:00
Tweak: New block Stoker: lights and puts out the boiler of a standing locomotive on signal (Input 0/1/2), Output says whether it burns. Reach set with a wrench. See the handbook.
Tweak: New block Signalman: reports the train on the rail in front of it (none/moving/standing) and the players aboard; Input sets the locomotive's power (1-10) or whistles (15). Wrench cycles the direction mode. See the handbook.
Tweak: New item Train Taxi Ticket: orders a conductor-driven train to a reachable station and brings it back to its timetable afterwards. See the handbook.
Tweak: The ticket used off a train calls the nearest fit train (conductor, passenger car, nobody aboard, fuel; loaded or off-screen) to a station within 50 blocks; limit TaxiSummonMaxBlocks in signalslink.json. See the handbook.
Tweak: The new blocks show a ghost preview and turn by where you aim when placing.
Tweak: The startup log says which of the new abilities this version of Yang's Transport Tycoon allows; each stands down on its own.
